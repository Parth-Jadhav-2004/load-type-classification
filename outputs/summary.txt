The selected model is Histogram gradient boosting. It was chosen for November macro-F1 of 0.600, before December was scored. On December 2018 the accuracy is 0.962, the macro-F1 is 0.958, and the weighted F1 is 0.961. Per-class F1 is light 0.968, medium 0.942, and maximum 0.965. Always predicting the majority training class reaches accuracy 0.586 and macro-F1 0.246 on the same month. Medium and maximum are swapped on 0 December intervals. November is harder than September, October, or December because it is the first month back on the winter weekday timetable after the fall timetable. December follows that same winter timetable, so the score rises once November is in the training set. The balanced boosting model is slightly higher on December and was not selected, because the choice was locked on November. NSM was replaced from the timestamp. Power factor was replaced from the meter formula wherever usage and reactive power made that possible. Median fills and the model itself were fit on January-November only.
